Rivet bundles third-party source from the projects listed below.

- Scintilla
  Upstream: https://www.scintilla.org/
  Source tree: third_party/scintilla
  Notice file: Scintilla.txt
- Lexilla
  Upstream: https://www.scintilla.org/Lexilla.html
  Source tree: third_party/lexilla
  Notice file: Lexilla.txt
- Rust crates for Windows x64, including build and development dependencies
  Versions: Cargo.lock
  Attribution and license texts: Rust-Crates.txt
  Regenerate with: pwsh -File scripts/generate-third-party-notices.ps1

Release packaging copies this entire directory into both:

- the portable zip produced by scripts/package.ps1
- the installer produced by installer/rivet.iss
